Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Assistant — last updated 27 October 2026.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool used only by its owner, Yusuf, with his own Google accounts. It is not distributed to the public.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent the app accesses the owner's Gmail messages, Google Calendar events and Google Drive files, and basic profile information (name and email address), in order to read, summarise and organise them on the owner's request.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally in files readable only by the owner.
  • Google user data is used only to provide the features the owner asks for. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train generalised AI models.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion and revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local token files. Questions: yqecea@gmail.com.

По-русски

Приложение личное, используется только владельцем со своими аккаунтами Google. Данные почты, календаря и диска обрабатываются на компьютере владельца, не передаются третьим лицам, не продаются и не используются для рекламы или обучения общих моделей. Доступ можно отозвать на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
