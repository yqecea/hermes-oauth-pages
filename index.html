Hermes Personal Assistant

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, single-user assistant that helps its owner, Yusuf, read and organise his own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive: summarise mail, add calendar events, and prepare documents.

The app runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to other people and does not collect data from other users.
